# Privacy policy

_UPSC Today · last updated 28 October 2026_

UPSC Today is a reading app for UPSC aspirants. It has no accounts, no ads and no login. This page lists
everything the app stores and sends, and why.

## On your phone only

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| The name you enter on first launch | To greet you | Stays on the phone |
| Stories you open, save or highlight, and the highlighted text | Read ticks, saved stories, highlights | Stays on the phone |
| Quiz answers you got wrong | The revision deck | Stays on the phone |
| Reading days and your daily goal | Streak, goal ring, reading calendar | Stays on the phone |
| Settings (paper mode, reminders) | Your preferences | Stays on the phone |

None of this is included in Android backups or device-to-device transfers. **Clear all** in the You tab
deletes saved stories and highlights; uninstalling the app deletes everything.

## Sent from the app

| What | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| A request for the day's feed | GitHub Pages (`ashusnapx.github.io`) | When the app opens or refreshes |
| Requests for article images | The coaching institute's image servers | When a story with a photo is shown |
| Anonymous feature counts (which features are used, quiz results) | Google Analytics | Always; **no install ID, no device details**, so a count cannot be traced to you |
| Detailed usage stats: the same events with an app-install ID, plus phone model, Android version, language and country | Google Analytics for Firebase | **Only if you choose "Share stats"** |

Detailed stats never include your name, your highlighted text, your phone's advertising ID or Android ID, and
ad personalisation is turned off. You can switch them off at any time in **You → Share detailed usage stats**,
which also resets the install ID. Google's handling of analytics data is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | Downloading the feed and images |
| Notifications | Up to three reminders a day and a Sunday review, each skipped when there's nothing worth saying |
| Run at startup | Re-arming those reminders after a reboot |
| Network state, wake lock | Used by Firebase Analytics to send stats efficiently |

## Who the app is for

UPSC Today is meant for adults preparing for the civil services examination.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/ashusnapx/news/issues. Changes to this policy are
recorded in this file's history.
