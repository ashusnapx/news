# Changelog

## 1.0 · 28 October 2026

First release, shared as an APK.

**Reading**
- The last 24 hours of current affairs from Vajiram & Ravi, Drishti IAS, Insights IAS and ForumIAS, refreshed hourly
- The same story from several coachings grouped into one, with a switch between versions
- Prelims and Mains tabs, GS1–GS4 filters, a top-stories carousel
- Full articles in-app: paper or screen mode, listen aloud, save, highlight, share as an image card
- Swipe between stories; full-screen images that rotate to fit

**Practice**
- Prelims Mode quiz from Vajiram & Ravi, IASbaba and Legacy IAS, with UPSC marking, option elimination,
  exam pace, streaks and explanations
- A revision deck that brings back missed questions after 1, 3 and 7 days

**Habit**
- Daily goal ring, reading streak, 12-week reading calendar
- Up to three reminders a day with a story carousel, plus a Sunday review

**Platform**
- Android 11 and later, phones to unfolded foldables and tablets, light and dark
- Opt-in detailed stats; anonymous feature counts
